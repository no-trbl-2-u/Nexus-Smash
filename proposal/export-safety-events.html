Count what stops the loop

Proposal: export Claude Code's claude_code.safety_event (2.1.307+) from CI ticks to a local sink, and count refusals per tick. Spec: proposal/export-safety-events.md. draft

ELI5

The robot works alone at night. Sometimes a guard says "no, you can't use that tool" and the robot quietly gives up on that part. In the morning nobody knows why less got done. Now the guard can drop a ticket in a box each time it says no. We put a box next to the robot, then count the tickets.
